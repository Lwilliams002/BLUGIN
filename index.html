A dining room on the ocean that becomes a lounge after dark.

Blu Gin sits in the round glass pavilion of the Castle Beach Club at 5445 Collins Avenue, with the pool at its feet and the Atlantic beyond. Breakfast from 8:30, lunch and dinner all day, and at ten the music comes up and the hookah is lit on the pool deck until midnight.

The kitchen is the Miami mix: whole Florida snapper, Cuban paella, Argentine churrasco with chimichurri, Italian pastas and thick Angus burgers. The bar keeps fifteen house cocktails, Francisco's wine list and the coldest beers on the beach.

Lounge nights at Blu Gin

At ten the tables stay and the room changes: the lights go blue, the music comes up, and the lounge runs until midnight, every night of the week.

  • Hookah on the pool deck or at the table, every flavor tobacco and nicotine free, from $35. Guests 21 and over.
  • Fifteen house cocktails, from Blu Gin on Collins to a smoked old fashioned over applewood, hickory, cherrywood or mesquite.
  • Francisco's wine list, bottles from $29.99 to Moët & Chandon, and the coldest beers on the beach.
  • Private events for parties of ten or more, with the whole pavilion and the pool deck available.
